# Motorly - Project Description

## Executive Summary

Motorly is a mobile application that serves as a personal AI-powered car mechanic assistant. The app empowers car owners to proactively manage their vehicle's health, diagnose issues, find trusted repair services, and maintain comprehensive service records—all through an intuitive mobile interface.

## Project Vision & Concept

### Core Idea
Motorly transforms the traditional car ownership experience by providing intelligent, proactive vehicle management. Instead of reacting to car problems when they occur, Motorly helps users prevent issues through regular maintenance tracking and provides instant AI-powered diagnosis when problems do arise.

### The Problem Motorly Solves
1. **Lack of Car Maintenance Knowledge**: Most car owners don't know when maintenance is due or what specific services their vehicle needs
2. **Difficulty Finding Trusted Mechanics**: Finding reliable, affordable auto repair shops is challenging and time-consuming
3. **Poor Service History Tracking**: Car owners often lose track of maintenance records, affecting resale value and warranty claims
4. **High Diagnostic Costs**: Traditional mechanic diagnostics can be expensive and time-consuming
5. **Fuel Cost Management**: Tracking fuel consumption and optimizing driving habits is difficult without proper tools

### The Motorly Solution
Motorly addresses these challenges through a comprehensive suite of features that make vehicle ownership smarter, more affordable, and less stressful.

## Target Audience

### Primary Users (End Users)
- **Individual Car Owners**: People who own one or more personal vehicles
- **Fleet Managers**: Small business owners managing multiple company vehicles
- **Car Enthusiasts**: Individuals who want detailed tracking of their vehicle's performance
- **New Drivers**: People who need guidance on vehicle maintenance

### Secondary Stakeholders
- **Auto Repair Shops**: Businesses that can list their services and offer promotions
- **Insurance Companies**: Potential partners for usage-based insurance programs
- **Car Dealerships**: Service departments looking to attract maintenance customers
- **Automotive Parts Retailers**: Companies offering maintenance products

## Core Features

### 1. AI-Powered Car Diagnosis
**Description**: Users can describe car problems in natural language and receive instant AI-generated diagnoses with likely issues, severity assessments, and recommended actions.

**Key Components**:
- **Chat Interface**: Conversational interface where users describe symptoms (e.g., "My car makes a grinding noise when I brake")
- **Diagnosis Cards**: AI-generated responses with structured information:
  - Likely issue identification
  - Severity assessment (Low/Medium/High)
  - Recommended immediate actions
  - Estimated repair urgency
- **Historical Context**: Previous diagnoses are saved for reference

**User Workflow**:
1. User navigates to Diagnose screen
2. Types description of car problem
3. Receives AI-generated diagnosis with severity assessment
4. Can ask follow-up questions for clarification
5. Diagnosis is saved to vehicle history

### 2. Vehicle Health Monitoring & Maintenance Tracking
**Description**: Comprehensive system for tracking vehicle health scores, upcoming maintenance, and service schedules.

**Key Components**:
- **Health Score Dashboard**: Overall vehicle health percentage with visual progress bar
- **Maintenance Items**: Track individual maintenance tasks with status indicators:
  - **Status Types**: Overdue, Due Soon, Scheduled, Good
  - **Due Indicators**: Mileage-based or date-based reminders
  - **Priority Levels**: Color-coded urgency indicators
- **Maintenance Categories**:
  - Routine maintenance (oil changes, tire rotations)
  - Component-specific maintenance (brakes, transmission)
  - Seasonal maintenance (winter preparation, AC service)

**User Workflow**:
1. View overall health score on Home screen
2. See upcoming maintenance items with status indicators
3. Tap any item for detailed information and actions
4. Mark items as completed when service is performed
5. Add new maintenance items as needed

### 3. Shop Discovery & Service Booking
**Description**: Find nearby auto repair shops with detailed information, ratings, and the ability to book services.

**Key Components**:
- **Shop Directory**: List of auto repair shops with filtering options
- **Shop Profiles**:
  - Name, address, contact information
  - Customer ratings and review counts
  - Distance from current location
  - Operating hours and availability status
  - Specialties and services offered
- **Location Integration**: Uses device location or manual address input
- **Booking System**: Interface for scheduling appointments (currently mocked)

**User Workflow**:
1. Browse shops by location, rating, or specialty
2. View detailed shop profiles
3. Check real-time availability
4. Book appointments for specific services
5. Save favorite shops for quick access

### 4. Special Offers & Promotions
**Description**: Exclusive deals and discounts from local auto repair shops and service providers.

**Key Components**:
- **Offer Cards**: Visually appealing cards showing current promotions
- **Offer Details**:
  - Promotion title and description
  - Valid dates and terms
  - Participating shop information
  - Claim process instructions
- **Categories**:
  - Percentage discounts (e.g., "20% Off Brake Service")
  - Free services (e.g., "Free Engine Health Scan")
  - Package deals (e.g., "Winter Preparation Package")

**User Workflow**:
1. Browse available offers on Home screen
2. Tap offer for detailed terms and conditions
3. Claim offer directly through the app
4. Receive confirmation and redemption instructions
5. Offers are saved to user account

### 5. Fuel Consumption Tracking
**Description**: Comprehensive system for logging fuel purchases, tracking consumption, and analyzing fuel costs.

**Key Components**:
- **Fuel Log Entries**: Record each fuel purchase with details:
  - Date and location
  - Liters purchased and price per liter
  - Total cost and odometer reading
  - Fuel station information
- **Consumption Analytics**:
  - Average fuel consumption (L/100km)
  - Cost per kilometer
  - Monthly and yearly fuel expenses
  - Consumption trends over time
- **Vehicle Comparison**: Compare fuel efficiency across different vehicles

**User Workflow**:
1. Add new fuel log after filling up
2. View all historical fuel logs
3. Analyze consumption patterns and costs
4. Identify opportunities for fuel savings
5. Export data for tax or expense reporting

### 6. Service History Management
**Description**: Complete record of all maintenance and repair services performed on each vehicle.

**Key Components**:
- **Service Records**: Detailed entries for each service:
  - Service type and description
  - Date performed and mileage at service
  - Shop name and location
  - Cost and payment information
  - Receipt/document storage
- **Categories**:
  - Routine maintenance
  - Repairs
  - Inspections
  - Part replacements
- **Document Management**: Store digital copies of receipts and invoices

**User Workflow**:
1. Add new service records after maintenance
2. Attach photos of receipts or invoices
3. View complete service history timeline
4. Filter by service type or date range
5. Generate reports for insurance or resale

### 7. Vehicle Profile Management
**Description**: Centralized management of all vehicle information and settings.

**Key Components**:
- **Vehicle Profiles**: Detailed information for each vehicle:
  - Make, model, year
  - License plate and VIN
  - Current mileage
  - Primary vehicle designation
- **Multiple Vehicle Support**: Manage multiple cars in one account
- **Profile Customization**: Vehicle-specific settings and preferences

**User Workflow**:
1. Add new vehicles with complete details
2. Set primary vehicle for quick access
3. Update mileage regularly
4. Switch between different vehicles
5. Remove vehicles when sold or transferred

### 8. User Account & Settings
**Description**: Personal account management and application preferences.

**Key Components**:
- **Profile Management**: Personal information and preferences
- **Notification Settings**: Customize alerts for maintenance, offers, etc.
- **Privacy Controls**: Manage data sharing preferences
- **Account Security**: Password management and security settings
- **Help & Support**: Access to FAQs and customer support

## Data Models & Information Architecture

### Core Data Entities

1. **Vehicle**
   - Basic information (make, model, year, VIN)
   - Current mileage and health score
   - Ownership details and primary status

2. **Maintenance Item**
   - Service type and description
   - Due criteria (mileage or date)
   - Current status and priority
   - Associated vehicle

3. **Service Record**
   - Service details and date performed
   - Shop information and cost
   - Mileage at service and receipts

4. **Fuel Log**
   - Purchase details (date, location, amount)
   - Price information and total cost
   - Odometer reading and consumption data

5. **Shop Profile**
   - Business information and contact details
   - Location data and operating hours
   - Services offered and specialties
   - Customer ratings and reviews

6. **Offer/Promotion**
   - Offer details and valid dates
   - Terms and conditions
   - Participating shop information
   - Redemption instructions

7. **Chat Message & Diagnosis**
   - User query and AI response
   - Diagnosis details and severity
   - Timestamp and conversation context

### Information Flow
- User data flows between local storage and cloud synchronization
- Vehicle data informs maintenance schedules and health scores
- Service records update vehicle history and affect health scores
- Fuel logs contribute to consumption analytics and cost tracking
- Shop data is sourced from external APIs and user contributions

## User Interface & Experience

### Design Philosophy
Motorly follows a clean, modern design with emphasis on:
- **Clarity**: Information is presented clearly without clutter
- **Actionability**: Each screen has clear next steps for users
- **Consistency**: Uniform design patterns across all screens
- **Accessibility**: Support for different abilities and preferences

### Visual Design Elements
- **Color Scheme**: Professional blues and neutrals with accent colors for alerts
- **Typography**: Clean, readable fonts (Manrope for headings, Inter for body)
- **Iconography**: Consistent icon set from Lucide React Native
- **Spacing & Layout**: Comfortable spacing with clear visual hierarchy
- **Animations**: Subtle animations for feedback and transitions

### Screen Layouts
1. **Home Screen**: Dashboard-style with key metrics and quick actions
2. **Diagnose Screen**: Chat interface focused on problem description
3. **Shops Screen**: Map and list view for shop discovery
4. **MyCar Screen**: Tabbed interface for maintenance, fuel, and service history
5. **Settings Screen**: Organized sections for account and preferences

## Technical Architecture (High-Level)

### Frontend Stack
- **Framework**: React Native with Expo for cross-platform development
- **Language**: TypeScript for type safety and maintainability
- **Navigation**: React Navigation for screen management
- **State Management**: React hooks and context for local state
- **Styling**: StyleSheet with design token system

### Key Technical Decisions
1. **Expo Managed Workflow**: Faster development with built-in native modules
2. **TypeScript**: Improved code quality and developer experience
3. **Component-Based Architecture**: Reusable, maintainable UI components
4. **Design Token System**: Centralized theming for consistent styling
